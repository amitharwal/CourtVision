Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data Collection

Court Vision does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking.

Local Storage

Your browser's local storage is used only to remember the players in your comparison list. It stays on your device. Other selections (season, filters, player) are kept in the page's address so links can be shared; nothing about them is stored.

Third-Party Services

Statistics come from the NBA's public stats API. They are fetched ahead of time and published with the site, so your browser never contacts stats.nba.com.

When you visit, your browser connects directly to these services, which receive your IP address and basic request details as part of each request:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site
  • The NBA's image CDN (cdn.nba.com), for player headshots and team logos
  • Google Fonts, for the site's typefaces
  • jsDelivr, which serves the Chart.js charting library on pages with charts

No other personal data is shared with them.

Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository.
