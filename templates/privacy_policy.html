Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

Data Collection

Court Vision does not collect any personal data. All NBA statistics are retrieved from public APIs.

Local Storage

We use browser local storage only for:

  • Saving your player comparison selections
  • Remembering your filter preferences

Third-Party Services

We use the NBA API to retrieve player and team statistics, the NBA's image CDN (cdn.nba.com) for player headshots and team logos, and Google Fonts for the site's typefaces. Your browser loads the images and fonts directly from those services, which receive your IP address as part of each request. No other personal data is shared with them.

Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository.
